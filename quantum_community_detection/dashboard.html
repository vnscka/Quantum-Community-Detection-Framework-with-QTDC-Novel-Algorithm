★ Novel Algorithm: Quantum Thermal Diffusion Community Detection (QTDC)
QTDC exploits the block structure of the quantum Gibbs thermal state ρ(β)=e−βH/Z at optimal β*. The blended Hamiltonian H=α·L−γ·B encodes both diffusion and modularity. A new quantity — Quantum Thermal Modularity (QTM) — locates β*. The Integrated Thermal Affinity (ITA) accumulates evidence across all temperatures, weighted by QTM.
H = α · L − γ · B (blended Hamiltonian) ρ(β) = e^{−βH} / Z(β) (Gibbs thermal state) QTM(β) = Tr(ρ(β) · B) / 2m (quantum generalized modularity) β* = argmax_β QTM(β) (optimal temperature) T_ij = Σ_β softmax[τ·QTM(β)] · |ρ(β)_ij| (Integrated Thermal Affinity)
Distinct from: quantum annealing (β→∞ ground state), deteQt/QSVT (single leading eigenvector), quantum walks (real-time e−iHt vs imaginary-time e−βH), and thermal TDA (Scali 2024, targets Betti numbers).
